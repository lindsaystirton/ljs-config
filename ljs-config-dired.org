** Dired-x

Dired extensions -- adds, among other things, =C-x C-j= (jump to
Dired at the current file) and =C-x C-q= (Dired's own wdired,
editable-filename mode already covers the common case, but dired-x's
extras are worth having loaded).

#+name: dired-x
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(use-package dired-x
  :straight nil
  :after dired)
#+end_src

** Casual Dired

A discoverable, Transient-based (pop-up menu) front end for Dired,
bound to =C-o=. Kept here with the rest of Dired's own config rather
than in ljs-config-discoverability.org, where the rest of the
casual/which-key discoverability work lives -- see the audit, §47/§48.

#+name: casual-dired
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(use-package casual-dired
  :after dired
  :bind (:map dired-mode-map ("C-o" . casual-dired-tmenu)))
#+end_src

** Open all marked files at once

Bound to =F= in a Dired buffer: visits every marked file (or the file
under point, or the next N files with a prefix argument) in one go.

#+name: dired-open-marked-files
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(with-eval-after-load 'dired
  (defun my-dired-find-file (&optional arg)
    "Open each of the marked files, or the file under the point, or
with a prefix arg, the next N files."
    (interactive "P")
    (mapc 'find-file (dired-get-marked-files nil arg)))
  (define-key dired-mode-map "F" 'my-dired-find-file))
#+end_src

* iBuffer

Manage buffers with =C-x C-b=, grouped by project/mode rather than as
one flat list.

#+name: ibuffer-custom
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(use-package ibuffer
  :straight nil
  :hook (ibuffer-mode . (lambda ()
                           (ibuffer-auto-mode 1)
                           (ibuffer-switch-to-saved-filter-groups "home")))
  :config
  (setq ibuffer-saved-filter-groups
        '(("home"
           ("emacs-config" (or (filename . ".emacs.d")
                                (filename . "emacs-config")))
           ("Org" (or (mode . org-mode)
                      (filename . "OrgMode")))
           ("Web Dev" (or (mode . html-mode)
                          (mode . css-mode)))
           ("Magit" (name . "\*magit"))
           ("ESS" (mode . ess-mode))
           ("LaTeX" (mode . latex-mode))
           ("Help" (or (name . "\*Help\*")
                       (name . "\*Apropos\*")
                       (name . "\*info\*"))))))
  (setq ibuffer-show-empty-filter-groups nil)
  (setq ibuffer-expert t))
#+end_src
