Part of [[file:ljs-config.org][LJS-Config]]. ESS settings borrowed heavily from Kieran Healy's [[https://github.com/kjhealy/emacs-starter-kit][emacs starter-kit for
the social sciences]]. I have used use-package to load and configure Yasnippet, and,
for in-buffer completion, Corfu and Cape (see below for why this replaced Company).

I have also added support for Vertico, Consult, Orderless, Marginalia (candidate annotations) and Embark (act-on-candidate). This was a tough call against Ivy, Counsel and Swiper, but the Vertico stack is what's actually installed and configured, so it's the settled choice.

** Install Consult
#+NAME: consult-installation
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(use-package consult
  :demand
  :bind (("C-s" . consult-line)
         ("M-g g" . consult-goto-line)
         ("M-g M-g" . consult-goto-line)
         ("C-x b" . consult-buffer)
         ("C-x 4 b" . consult-buffer-other-window)
         ("C-x 5 b" . consult-buffer-other-frame)
         ("M-y" . consult-yank-pop)
         ("C-x r b" . consult-bookmark)
         ("C-x M-f" . consult-find)
         :map vertico-map
         ("C-j" . vertico-next)
         ("C-k" . vertico-previous))
  :config
  (setq consult-line-point-position 'window)
  ;; Uses Emacs's built-in project.el rather than the (uninstalled)
  ;; projectile package, which the old config referenced but never
  ;; actually depended on -- this used to be a latent error waiting
  ;; to happen the first time a project-scoped consult command ran.
  (setq consult-line-prompts
        `(,buffer-file-name
          ,(when-let ((proj (project-current)))
             (file-name-nondirectory (directory-file-name (project-root proj))))
          ,(abbreviate-file-name default-directory)))
  :custom
  (consult-project-root-function
   (lambda () (when-let ((proj (project-current))) (project-root proj))))
  (consult-narrow-key "<"))
#+end_src

** Icomplete (removed)

Icomplete used to be turned on here alongside Vertico. Running both at
once is redundant -- they are two different minibuffer completion UIs
-- and can visually conflict, so this has been removed. Vertico
supersedes it.




** Install Marginalia and Embark

Marginalia annotates completion candidates in the minibuffer (docstrings
for =M-x=, file details for =find-file=, etc). Embark lets you act on a
candidate directly from the minibuffer or at point.

NOTE: marginalia-mode was temporarily switched off (see git history
for this file) after its freshest MELPA snapshot crashed the file
annotator -- `marginalia--time-relative' called the built-in
`seconds-to-string' with three arguments, which Emacs 30.1's version
didn't accept. Re-enabled here to test whether the Emacs 31.1 upgrade
resolved the mismatch. If `find-file' or `M-x' start erroring again,
re-comment `(marginalia-mode)' below and reopen this as still broken.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp :tangle yes
(use-package marginalia
  :config
  (marginalia-mode))
#+end_src

* Starter kit yasnippet

Yasnippet is a snippet expansion system for Emacs. It uses short phrases and the tab key to trigger the insertion of frequently-used snippets of code or text in your document. 

** Load the yasnippet bundle
#+begin_src emacs-lisp :tangle yes
(use-package yasnippet
  :config
  (yas-global-mode 1)
  
  ;; Load snippets from the package's ./snippets/ directory
  (let ((snippets-dir (expand-file-name "snippets" dotfiles-dir)))
    (when (file-directory-p snippets-dir)
      (yas-load-directory snippets-dir)))

  ;; Check expansion function
  (defun check-expansion ()
    (save-excursion
      (if (looking-at "\\_>") t
        (backward-char 1)
        (if (looking-at "\\.") t
          (backward-char 1)
          (if (looking-at "->") t nil)))))

  ;; Do yas-expand function
  (defun do-yas-expand ()
    (let ((yas-fallback-behavior 'return-nil))
      (yas-expand)))

  ;; Tab, indent, or complete function
  (defun tab-indent-or-complete ()
    (interactive)
    (if (minibufferp)
        (minibuffer-complete)
      (if (or (not yas-minor-mode)
              (null (do-yas-expand)))
          (if (check-expansion)
              (completion-at-point)
            (indent-for-tab-command)))))

  ;; Bind tab to tab-indent-or-complete
  :bind
  (:map yas-minor-mode-map
        ([tab] . tab-indent-or-complete)))  
#+end_src

** Corfu, Cape, and in-buffer completion (replaces Company)

Company-mode was the in-buffer completion popup here for a long time,
but it's a separate, older protocol from Emacs's built-in
=completion-at-point-functions= (capf) mechanism. Corfu is the
in-buffer counterpart to Vertico -- same author, same minimalist
philosophy, and it displays whatever comes through the standard capf
mechanism rather than requiring its own backend protocol. Most of what
we relied on Company for (ESS/R, elisp, AUCTeX macros) already goes
through capf natively via each major mode, so Corfu picks it up with
no extra wiring. The two backends that were Company-specific --
company-math and company-stan (see ljs-config-stats.org) -- are kept,
not dropped: Cape's =cape-company-to-capf= wraps a Company backend so
it can still be used as a capf source under Corfu.

#+source: corfu-mode
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(use-package corfu
  :custom
  (corfu-cycle t)
  (corfu-auto t)
  (corfu-auto-prefix 1)
  (corfu-auto-delay 0)
  (corfu-quit-no-match 'separator)
  :bind
  (("<C-tab>" . completion-at-point)
   :map corfu-map
   ("C-n" . corfu-next)
   ("C-p" . corfu-previous)
   ("TAB" . corfu-complete)
   ("<tab>" . corfu-complete)
   ("<backtab>" . corfu-previous))
  :init
  (global-corfu-mode))
#+end_src

#+source: cape-mode
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(use-package cape
  :init
  ;; General-purpose fallback sources, added at a low priority (depth
  ;; 100) so they only kick in after any mode-specific capf. This is
  ;; the rough equivalent of company's old :with dabbrev behaviour
  ;; (completion from buffer/other-buffer text).
  (add-hook 'completion-at-point-functions #'cape-dabbrev 100)
  (add-hook 'completion-at-point-functions #'cape-file 100)
  (add-hook 'completion-at-point-functions #'cape-elisp-block 100))
#+end_src

#+source: company-math-bridge
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
;; company-math has no native capf backend, so bridge it via Cape
;; instead of dropping it. Registered globally, matching its old
;; global company-backends registration.
(use-package company-math
  :commands (company-math-symbols-unicode)
  :config
  (add-hook 'completion-at-point-functions
            (cape-company-to-capf #'company-math-symbols-unicode)))
#+end_src
